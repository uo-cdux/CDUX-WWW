Some notes on dealing with Hank

I would like to think that I am actually pretty good on following through on things. But it is no secret that my schedule is packed full, and things do drop occasionally. Below are some tips on how to manage your advisor:

  • The best way to guarantee f2f meetings is by consulting my calendar and proposing to meet at an open time.
  • Never come early to a meeting in my office. (I use the time between meetings to triage emails.) If you are a minute or two late, that is always OK.
  • If you wrote an email and have gotten no response for 24 hours, always feel free to re-send.
  • If I say I will do something, it is always OK to send an email reminder. Even minutes afterwards.
  • If I am late to a meeting (often because the previous meeting ran long), please do not sit outside my door. I will come to your office and collect you when I return to my office. If you are concerned that I forgot about the meeting, then email me or text me.
