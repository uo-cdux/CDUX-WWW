Research Challenges for Visualization Software

Summary

Over the last twenty-five years, visualization software has evolved into robust frameworks that can be used for research projects, rapid prototype development, or as the basis of richly featured, end-user tools. Our group took stock of current capabilities and considered upcoming challenges facing visualization software. Our findings were published in an article in Computer Magazine, which has a readership of 55,000. We also presented our findings at a panel at the IEEE Visualization conference in Atlanta, GA.

We identified six key challenges: massive parallelism, processor architecture and programming model, application architecture and data management, data model, rendering, and interaction. For each challenge, we identified what had happened so far, what the issues were going forward, and speculated whether evolutionary or revolutionary change was needed. To learn more, read the article.
