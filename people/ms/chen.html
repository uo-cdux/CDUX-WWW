Bio

Vincent is a second year M.S. Student in Computer Science at the University of Oregon. Vincent graduated from University of Toronto with a BASc in Computer / Electrical Engineering. His research interests reside in computer graphics, GPU computing, and scientific visualization.

Previously, Vincent researched real-time 3D scanning techniques with Professor Song Zhang at Purdue University, as well as taking part in a 3D printing social startup with Professor Matt Ratto from University of Toronto.
