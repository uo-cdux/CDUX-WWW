Bio

Yuhang Liang is currently a Ph.D. student in the Computer Science Department of the University of Oregon, advised by Prof. Jieyang Chen. He received his Master's degree in Data Science from the University of Auckland in 2023 and his Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Southwest University in 2021. His research interests include high-performance computing, fault tolerance, GPU computing, and machine learning.
