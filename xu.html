Bio

Hang Xu is a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Oregon. Hang joined CDUX in September 2013. He will research visualization techniques on the Intel Xeon Phi in the context of the VisIt visualization program, with foci on cache usage and vector utilization. He is advised by Dr. Hank Childs.
